# The Zero-Extraction DAO Economic Architecture

The 100% flow-through economic model benefits the DAO by functioning as a pure conduit rather than a traditional profit-aggregating corporation, creating several structural and competitive advantages:

\*   \*\*Maximum Incentive Alignment:\*\* By automatically allocating 100% of all FIAT revenue directly to the participants who power, grow, manage, and secure the network (Node Operators, Sales Sources, Affiliate trees, the Steward, and the Infrastructure Manager), every role is financially rewarded and highly motivated \[1-3\].
\*   \*\*Prevention of Centralized Financial Power:\*\* Because the DAO retains zero profit by design, the model ensures there is no accumulation of corporate wealth, no treasury extraction, and no centralized financial power center \[4\].
\*   \*\*Elimination of Corporate Extraction and Overhead:\*\* This flow-through structure is a core competitive advantage that removes corporate extraction, traditional overhead, and corporate payroll burdens, allowing the network to remain highly competitive \[2, 3\].

Ultimately, the model ensures that the DAO serves strictly as an economic organism where every dollar is accounted for and distributed directly to its owners and stakeholders \[3-5\].
