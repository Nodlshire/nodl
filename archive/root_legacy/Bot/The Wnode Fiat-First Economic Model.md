# The Wnode Fiat-First Economic Model

Node operators in the Wnode ecosystem, known as Nodlrs, get paid in USD through a "Fiat-First" economic model that utilizes \*\*Stripe Connect\*\* for all financial settlements \[1-3\]. 

Here is exactly how the payout process works:
\*   \*\*Customer Payments:\*\* Enterprise clients and developers purchase compute resources on the network primarily using stable fiat currency (USD) \[1, 4\]. 
\*   \*\*Revenue Split:\*\* For every dollar that enters the mesh from a compute buyer, \*\*70% of that fiat revenue is distributed directly to the node operator\*\* who provided the compute capacity \[5-7\]. 
\*   \*\*Stripe Integration:\*\* The entire payout engine—including billing, payouts, financial settlement, and compliance reporting—is handled seamlessly by Stripe Connect \[1, 2\]. Stripe also manages the necessary AML (Anti-Money Laundering) and KYC (Know Your Customer) verifications during the operator's initial onboarding process, allowing the network to scale without friction \[1, 3\].

\*\*The Strategic Advantage of Fiat Payouts\*\*
By paying operators directly in USD rather than relying on cryptocurrency, Wnode ensures that participants receive \*\*stable, predictable, and real-world income\*\* that is fully protected from the price volatility often associated with speculative token markets \[1, 3, 4\]. 

While fiat is the primary and most dependable revenue stream, operators can additionally earn optional WNODE token bonuses based on their node's performance, uptime, and reliability, creating a powerful dual-incentive structure \[4, 7\].
