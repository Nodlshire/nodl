# Wnode Affiliate Tree — How It Works

## Overview
The Wnode Affiliate Tree is a two‑level referral system that rewards people for expanding the Wnode mesh.  
Every participant has:
- A **Level 1** (direct referrals)
- A **Level 2** (referrals made by your Level 1)

The tree grows automatically as new users join through referral links.  
Earnings scale with the size and activity of your tree.

---

## Commission Percentages
The Wnode affiliate system pays out:

- **3% on Level 1** (your direct referrals)
- **7% on Level 2** (referrals made by your Level 1)

These percentages apply to:
- Node purchases  
- Compute activity  
- Ongoing network participation  

This creates a simple, transparent, high‑leverage earning model.

---

## Structure of the Affiliate Tree

### Level 1 — Direct Referrals (3%)
These are people who join Wnode using **your** referral link.  
You earn:
- **3% commission** on their node purchases  
- **3% of their compute activity**  
- **3% of their ongoing network participation**  

### Level 2 — Indirect Referrals (7%)
These are people who join using the links of your Level 1 members.  
You earn:
- **7% commission** on their node purchases  
- **7% of their compute activity**  
- **7% of their ongoing network participation**  

This makes Level 2 extremely powerful — it is the first layer of **organic, self‑propagating growth**.

---

## How the Tree Grows
1. You invite someone → they become **your Level 1**  
2. They invite someone → that person becomes **your Level 2**  
3. Any further levels belong to the people below you  
4. Your tree expands automatically as people continue inviting  

This is **not MLM**:
- Only **two earning levels**  
- No infinite depth  
- No stacking  
- No forced purchases  

---

## Earnings Model

### 1. Node Purchases
When someone in your tree buys a node:
- You earn **3%** if they are Level 1  
- You earn **7%** if they are Level 2  

### 2. Compute Activity
As nodes in your tree run jobs:
- You earn **3%** from Level 1  
- You earn **7%** from Level 2  

This creates **ongoing passive income**.

### 3. Tree‑Wide Bonus (If Applicable)
Some roles (e.g., Founder Slots) may receive additional network‑wide participation bonuses.

---

## Founder Slots (If Applicable)
Founder Slots are special positions in the affiliate tree with enhanced rights:
- Higher commission multipliers  
- Larger share of network‑wide compute activity  
- Priority in early‑stage rewards  
- Rights to a “live” tree that continues growing even after transfer  

Founder Slots are limited and treated as **economic assets**.

---

## Key Properties
- The tree is **self‑propagating**  
- Earnings grow as the network grows  
- You benefit from activity you did not personally recruit  
- The system is **transparent**, **deterministic**, and **non‑extractive**  
- The affiliate tree accelerates Wnode adoption and compute supply  

---

## Summary
The Wnode Affiliate Tree is a simple, two‑level referral system that:
- Pays **3% on Level 1**  
- Pays **7% on Level 2**  
- Generates ongoing passive income from compute activity  
- Scales automatically as the mesh expands  
- Supports special Founder Slots with enhanced rights  

It is designed to be:
- Fair  
- Transparent  
- Easy to understand
