The Problem

Compute demand is exploding due to AI agents and autonomous systems, yet current solutions fall short:
•

Centralised Cloud Limitations: High costs, opaque pricing, vendor lock-in, single points of failure, and significant
environmental impact.

•

Idle Hardware Waste: Consumer and enterprise devices sit idle ~75% of the time.

•

DePIN Fragmentation: Most networks are vertical-specific, token-heavy, technically complex, and lack unified
agent execution or regulated fiat rails.

•

Lack of True Redundancy: No major network spans dense terrestrial coverage and orbital/sovereign execution.

The result is a market failing to serve the granular, high-frequency, verifiable compute needs of the agent economy.

4.

Architecture

Wnode consists of heterogeneous node types (Low, Standard, Pro, Edge, Enterprise, and future Space Nodes) feeding into a
distributed execution environment.
4.1

Key Technical Components:
•

Sovereign AI Orchestrator: Intelligent routing engine that assigns micro-jobs based on locality, performance,
reputation, real-time telemetry, and market conditions. Uses Mixture-of-Experts patterns for self-optimization.

•

Execution Layer: RAM-only, encrypted, deterministic. Zero persistent storage on nodes.

•

Verification Layer: Redundant execution, hash matching, deterministic replay, and cryptographic proofs.

•

Security Model: Hardware fingerprinting, automatic isolation, no plaintext exposure.

This architecture excels at fast, granular workloads where persistence is unnecessary or handled externally via protocol
integrations.
