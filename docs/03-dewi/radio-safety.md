# RF Safety & Regulatory Compliance

1. **FCC Part 15 / Part 96 Compliance**: Automatic Effective Isotropic Radiated Power (EIRP) limiting.
2. **Specific Absorption Rate (SAR)**: Enforces maximum RF exposure limits (MPE) for residential and commercial indoor gateway installations.
3. **Dynamic Spectrum Access (SAS)**: CBRS nodes interface with Spectrum Access Systems to prevent interference with radar installations.
