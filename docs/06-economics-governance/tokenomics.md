# WWEX Tokenomics & Staking Mechanics

## 1. Token Distribution
* **Total Supply**: 1,000,000,000 WWEX
* **Node Operator Mining & Staking Rewards**: 45%
* **Protocol Ecosystem & Growth**: 25%
* **Founding Team & Contributors**: 15% (36-month linear vest)
* **DAO Treasury**: 15%

## 2. Buyback-and-Burn Mechanism
100% of network compute purchasing fees collected in USD are used to perform market buyback and burn of WWEX tokens, creating deflationary pressure linked directly to real-world compute demand.
