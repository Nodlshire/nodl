# Enterprise Automation

Wnode provides enterprise-grade infrastructure for automated compliance, treasury management, and sovereign compute operations.

## Institutional Settlement
By integrating Tether (USDT and XAUt), Wnode offers enterprises a robust, compliant settlement rail.

- **Automated Compliance**: Enterprises can leverage identity compliance workflows to enforce KYC/KYB requirements programmatically on direct issuances and large M2M flows.
- **On/Off-Ramp Mechanics**: Streamlined integration with Tether's issuance and redemption models ensures enterprises can efficiently move large volumes of fiat or gold on and off the ledger.
- **Auditability**: Real-time RPC adapters fetch reserve attestations, providing enterprises with mathematical certainty of backing models (cash, T-bills, vaulted gold) without relying on manual audits.
