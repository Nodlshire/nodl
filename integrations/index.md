# Wnode Integration Index

Wnode serves as the sovereign compute mesh for AI agents, DePIN, and M2M financial workflows. Our integration registry connects the machine economy to institutional-grade liquidity and settlement rails.

## Core Settlement Infrastructure

### Tether (USDT & XAUt)
Tether is integrated as a first-class protocol within the Wnode ecosystem, serving as the primary fiat-backed stablecoin and gold-backed settlement rail. 

- **USDT Networks**: Natively supported across Ethereum, Tron, Polygon, and Base for high-frequency, cross-border M2M payments.
- **Tether Gold (XAUt)**: Integrated as a premier Real-World Asset (RWA) backed by physically vaulted gold, providing a stable, inflation-resistant treasury asset for autonomous machine networks.
- **Compliance & Attestations**: Full support for identity compliance, including KYC/KYB issuance, onchain sanction enforcement, and transparent reserve attestations.
