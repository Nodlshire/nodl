# MEV Integration

## Architecture
MEV (Maximum Extractable Value) on Ethereum relies on a Proposer-Builder Separation (PBS) model via MEV-Boost. Block builders construct blocks maximizing profit, and relays validate these blocks before forwarding them to validators. Wnode integrates directly with major relays to submit transaction bundles securely, bypassing the public mempool to prevent front-running.

## Supported Relays & Builders
- Flashbots (https://relay.flashbots.net)
- bloXroute (https://mev.api.bloxroute.com)
- Beaverbuild
- Titan Builder

## How Wnode uses MEV
Wnode utilizes this integration for the automated protection of M2M agent payments and to capture arbitrage opportunities (direct MEV revenue) generated by protocol interactions on the platform.

## Required Endpoints
- `eth_sendBundle` RPC method required at the relay endpoint.
