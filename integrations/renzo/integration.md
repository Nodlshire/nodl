# Renzo Protocol Integration Overview

## What is Renzo?
Renzo is a Liquid Restaking Token (LRT) and Strategy Manager built on EigenLayer. Users deposit ETH or LSTs (like stETH) to mint ezETH. Renzo abstracts the complex process of securing Actively Validated Services (AVSs), automatically routing deposits to the highest-yielding EigenLayer operators.

## Why Wnode Integrates with Renzo
Wnode integrates with Renzo to automate the yield farming of EigenLayer points and AVS rewards, and to position Wnode operators as whitelisted infrastructure providers for Renzo's massive delegated TVL.

## How Wnode Interacts with Renzo
Wnode interfaces with the `RestakeManager` to automate the minting of ezETH. Additionally, the Wnode infrastructure layer integrates directly with Renzo's operator delegation registry.

## Example Agent Workflows
- **Yield Arbitrage & Auto-Farming**: Wnode monitors the secondary market peg of ezETH/ETH on DEXes. If ezETH trades at a slight discount, Wnode buys it from the DEX rather than minting via the protocol, maximizing point accumulation and staking yields.
- **Operator Node Deployment**: Wnode spins up AVS containers (e.g., EigenDA) specifically to accept restaked ETH delegated from the Renzo protocol treasury.

## Revenue Model (Real Incentives)
Wnode generates revenue via **Restaking Yield & Operator Commissions**:
1. **ezETH Yield**: Earning native Ethereum staking rewards plus AVS token airdrops/yields.
2. **Renzo Delegation Fees**: As a whitelisted operator, Wnode earns a 5-10% commission on the AVS yield generated by the ETH delegated by Renzo users.

## Activation Steps
1. Configure Wnode auto-restaking agents to target the `RestakeManager` contract.
2. Pass Renzo DAO governance proposals to become a whitelisted Node Operator.
3. Set `ENABLE_RENZO_AUTOMATION=true`.

## Limitations
- LRTs carry stacked smart contract risk (Ethereum Base -> EigenLayer -> Renzo -> AVS). If any layer suffers a vulnerability or slashing event, the ezETH token could de-peg.

## Future Upgrade Path
- Automated points-hedging: Shorting ezETH points on markets like Whales Market or Pendle Finance while simultaneously farming them via Wnode deposits to lock in risk-free USD yields.
