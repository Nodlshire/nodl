# Rocket Pool Integration Overview

## What is Rocket Pool?
Rocket Pool is a decentralized liquid staking protocol for Ethereum. It allows users to stake ETH and receive rETH. Node operators join the network by depositing 8 (or 16) ETH plus RPL tokens as insurance collateral, creating a "minipool" that is paired with user ETH to form a full 32 ETH validator.

## Why Wnode Integrates with Rocket Pool
Wnode is inherently designed to run decentralized compute and validation workloads. Integrating with Rocket Pool allows Wnode operators to act as Ethereum validators with significantly lower capital requirements (8 ETH instead of 32 ETH) while earning massive commission multipliers.

## How Wnode Interacts with Rocket Pool
Wnode deploys the official Rocket Pool Smartnode stack (or orchestrates native consensus/execution containers). It interacts with the `RocketNodeManager` and `RocketMinipoolManager` contracts to register nodes, deposit ETH, and claim periodic RPL rewards.

## Example Agent Workflows
- **Minipool Provisioning**: Wnode detects a deposit of 8 ETH and sufficient RPL collateral in the operator wallet. It automatically spins up the Ethereum validator containers, generates keys, and executes the `deposit()` function to initiate the minipool queue.
- **Collateral Rebalancing (RPL)**: Wnode monitors the operator's RPL collateralization ratio. If the ratio drops near the minimum threshold (10%), it automatically claims accrued RPL rewards and restakes them to avoid reward penalties.

## Revenue Model (Real Incentives)
Wnode generates revenue via **Node Operator Commissions**:
1. **Consensus & Execution Yield**: Operators receive 100% of the yield on their own 8 ETH, plus a commission (typically 14%) on the yield generated by the 24 ETH provided by the protocol.
2. **RPL Inflation Rewards**: Node operators earn monthly RPL token emissions based on their staked collateral weight.

## Activation Steps
1. Acquire 8 ETH and the required minimum RPL collateral (10% of borrowed ETH value).
2. Configure Wnode hardware for robust 24/7 uptime (2TB NVMe SSD, 32GB RAM).
3. Set `ENABLE_ROCKETPOOL_SMARTNODE=true` to deploy the validator stack.

## Limitations
- Requires active ETH and RPL market price monitoring. If the RPL token crashes relative to ETH, the collateralization ratio may drop below 10%, halting RPL reward issuance until collateral is topped up.

## Future Upgrade Path
