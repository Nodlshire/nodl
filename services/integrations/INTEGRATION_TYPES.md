# Wnode Integration Ecosystem — Category Overview

Wnode maintains a rapidly expanding registry of integrations across Web3, AI, cloud, enterprise SaaS, developer tooling, and financial infrastructure.  
Each integration represents a potential compute consumer ready to activate on the Wnode sovereign compute network.

This document provides a structured overview of the integration categories currently supported.

## 1. Layer‑1 Blockchains
Foundational base-layer networks requiring RPC, indexing, and compute services.

Examples: Solana, Avalanche, NEAR, Algorand, Filecoin, Arweave, Polkadot, Kusama, Mina, Oasis.

## 2. Layer‑2 & Rollup Ecosystems
High‑throughput scaling networks with heavy RPC and indexing demand.

Examples: Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, zkSync, Scroll, Linea, StarkNet, Mantle, Blast, Mode, Taiko.

## 3. Cross‑Chain & Interoperability Protocols
Bridges, messaging layers, and interoperability frameworks.

Examples: Wormhole, LayerZero, Axelar, Celer, Gravity Bridge, Router Protocol, Nomad.

## 4. Indexers & Data Protocols
High‑volume data consumers ideal for decentralized compute.

Examples: The Graph, SubQuery, Dune, Flipside, Footprint, TokenTerminal, Nansen, Glassnode.

## 5. DeFi Protocols
AMMs, lending markets, derivatives, and structured products.

Examples: Uniswap, Aave, Curve, Balancer, GMX, Synthetix, Pendle, Gearbox, Ribbon, Lyra.

## 6. CEX, Wallets & Custody
Exchanges, wallets, and custody providers requiring secure compute.

Examples: Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, BitGo, Fireblocks, Ledger, Trezor, Phantom, Rabby.

## 7. Storage & CDN
Decentralized and centralized storage networks.

Examples: IPFS, Filebase, Storj, Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, Ar.io, Bundlr.

## 8. AI & ML Platforms
Inference APIs, model hosting, and agentic compute consumers.

Examples: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Vertex, Cohere, HuggingFace, Replicate, AWS Bedrock.

## 9. Cloud Providers & Compute Platforms
General compute, serverless, and container orchestration.

Examples: AWS, Azure, GCP, DigitalOcean, Linode, Vultr, Hetzner, Render, Fly.io.

## 10. Databases & Data Engineering
Managed databases, warehouses, and streaming systems.

Examples: MongoDB Atlas, Postgres Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks, ClickHouse, Kafka, Redshift.

## 11. Observability & Monitoring
Metrics, logs, traces, and distributed telemetry.

Examples: Datadog, New Relic, Grafana Cloud, Prometheus, Loki, Tempo, Jaeger, Honeycomb.

## 12. Identity, Security & Zero‑Trust
Authentication, authorization, and secure access.

Examples: Auth0, Okta, Azure AD, Cloudflare Zero Trust, Duo, Tailscale, 1Password.

## 13. Payments, Banking & Fintech
Payment processors, neobanks, and financial APIs.

Examples: Stripe, Adyen, Square, Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, Marqeta, Plaid, Teller.

## 14. Enterprise SaaS
CRM, ERP, HRIS, accounting, and productivity suites.

Examples: Salesforce, HubSpot, Workday, NetSuite, Xero, FreshBooks, Sage.

## 15. Developer Tools & CI/CD
Build pipelines, automation, and developer productivity.

Examples: GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, CircleCI, Jenkins, Argo, Terraform Cloud, Pulumi.

## 16. Messaging & Communication
Real‑time messaging, email, and notifications.

Examples: Slack, Discord, Telegram, Twilio, SendGrid, Mailgun, Postmark.

## 17. Collaboration & Productivity
Design, documentation, and workflow tools.

Examples: Figma, Miro, Notion, Confluence, Jira, Asana, ClickUp, Linear.
